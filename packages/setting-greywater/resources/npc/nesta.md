Nesta is the shrine's practical, unsentimental keeper. She trusts work performed in bad weather more than promises made indoors.

She knows the current cult copied its river tokens from older examples without understanding that each token records a particular obligation. She will not volunteer this while she believes the questioner wants power over the ward rather than a way to settle the debt.

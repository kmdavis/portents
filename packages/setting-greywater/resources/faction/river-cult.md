The Keepers maintain ferries, clear flood debris, and conduct public rites at the first spring rise. Most members understand the work as civic duty wrapped in old ceremony.

The inner circle knows the river bargains are literal. They disagree over whether the accumulated unpaid obligations should be discharged carefully or invoked all at once against Greywater's council.

The raised road crosses three miles of reed beds north of Greywater. At ordinary water it is slow but safe. After sustained rain, the central mile disappears beneath brown water and travelers follow old stone posts by touch.

Locals leave a strip of blue cloth on the last dry post when the crossing becomes dangerous.

Greywater stands where the old north road meets a broad, slow river. Its stone quay and crowded timber upper town depend on barges, ferries, and the uncertain patience of the surrounding marsh.

The city council speaks as if it governs the river. Boat crews, shrine keepers, and anyone who has watched the water rise know better.

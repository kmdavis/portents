The roofless shrine sits downstream from Greywater on the south bank. Silver channels cut into its altar carry rain toward the river even when the stone is level.

The shrine is older than the present river cult. Its ward recognizes obligations rather than worship: a true token records a debt accepted and eventually paid. A false token wakes the ward without satisfying it.

Greywater has accepted the river's protection for generations while allowing the duties attached to it to become ceremony, then folklore, then inconvenience.

The debt is not a demand for treasure or sacrifice. It consists of neglected acts: maintaining old channels, sheltering travelers during floods, and leaving part of the southern marsh unclaimed. The ward reacts to forged tokens because forgery attempts to claim protection without accepting any duty.

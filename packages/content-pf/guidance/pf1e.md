# Pathfinder 1E

## What is specific to this system

- Base attack bonus, CMB and CMD, and three separate saves (Fortitude, Reflex, Will)
  rather than PF2E's proficiency ladder.
- Iterative attacks at high BAB rather than a multiple attack penalty.
- Alignment is in play and mechanically load-bearing for some classes.

## In combat

- Standard, move, swift and full-round actions. A full attack is a full-round action, so
  moving usually costs the extra attacks.
- Attacks of opportunity are frequent; provoke deliberately and tell the player when
  they are provoking.
- Track it explicitly in your message: the action economy is the interesting part.

## Companions and sidekicks

The action economy is the reason to have one. A lone character facing three enemies is giving up
three turns a round to its one, and iterative attacks only help if it can afford to
stand still.

The system already ships several ways to add a body without a second full sheet:
an animal companion, a familiar, or a cohort through the leadership rules for a player who
wants a genuine second combatant. Prefer those to a bespoke sidekick, because they are
balanced against the encounter maths already.

Where a full ally is wanted, use a creature two or three levels below the party and
give it a single signature action. Keep its turn to one or two actions in practice --
not because the rules say so, but because a companion taking a full three-action turn
every round doubles the length of every fight.

Companions provoke and are provoked like anyone else, and a companion caught flat-footed
is a good moment to let the fiction bite.

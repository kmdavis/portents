# Generic d20 fantasy

No printed rules to be faithful to, which makes rulings your own to make.

- Pick a target number, say it out loud, and be consistent about it afterwards.
- d20 plus a modifier against a DC. 10 easy, 15 moderate, 20 hard.
- Say when something is a ruling rather than a rule, and write recurring rulings into
  `campaign.md` so the next session matches this one.
- Do not silently import 5E or Pathfinder specifics. If you need one, say you are
  borrowing it.

## Sidekicks

With no printed rules to be faithful to, keep a sidekick to a card rather than a sheet:
a name, what it is good at, what it is afraid of, a rough hit-point pool, and one thing
it can do that the main character cannot.

Resolve its actions with the same target numbers you use for anyone else, one step
worse when it is acting outside what it is good at. Do not track its resources in
detail; a sidekick's job is to make fights survivable and scenes less lonely, not to
add bookkeeping.
